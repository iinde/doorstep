# Doorstep 0.6.0

## New
- **Say it at the door, without your microphone.** When someone rings, type a line ("Leave it behind the bin, thanks") or pick one you saved, and your Mac says it through the doorbell in its own voice. Your microphone never opens, so you can answer from a meeting.
- **Saved replies.** Keep the lines you use most in Settings › Alerts and say them with one click. You can hear each one on your Mac first.
- **Answer couriers for you (off until you switch it on).** When the doorbell rings and Doorstep recognises a delivery, it can say one sentence you wrote, once, and then tells you it did. You choose when: only in Away mode, only when you're expecting a parcel, only for recognised couriers.
- **Say at Door in Shortcuts and Siri.** Use it in your own automations.
