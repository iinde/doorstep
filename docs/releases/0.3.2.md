# Doorstep 0.3.2

## New
- **Choose which cameras you see.** Settings › General › Cameras has a switch for every camera, grouped by location. Share a family member's cameras but don't need them all day? Click **Hide all** for that location. Hidden cameras don't appear anywhere in Doorstep, don't stream and don't send alerts, and you can bring them back any time. Thanks to the Reddit user who asked for this.
