# Doorstep 0.7.0

## New
- **One window for everything.** Live, History, Map, Radar and Insights now sit together in one Doorstep window, with a rail down the side to switch between them (⌘1 to ⌘5). The separate History and Command Centre windows are gone.
- **Light mode.** Doorstep now follows your Mac's appearance. Prefer one look all the time? Choose it in Settings › General › Appearance.
- **A proper welcome.** The first time you open the window, Doorstep shows you where everything is and offers the few choices most people want.

## Better
- History no longer keeps your cameras awake while you browse old visits.
- Settings reopens on the tab you last used.
