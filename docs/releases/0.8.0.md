# Doorstep 0.8.0

## New
- **Other cameras.** Reolink, Tapo, Hikvision, Dahua, Amcrest, UniFi and most ONVIF or RTSP cameras now show in Live next to your Ring cameras, straight from the camera to your Mac. Doorstep can find them on your network and tell you when one sees a person.
- **Ask your door.** Type a question in History, like "did DPD come today?" or "when did the postie come?", and get a one-line answer with the visits underneath.
- **While you were out.** Come back to your Mac after a while and a small card shows the visits, rings and parcels you missed.
- **Quiet on calls.** While any app is using your microphone or camera, alerts arrive silently and nothing is said out loud.
- **Offline alerts.** Doorstep tells you straight away when a camera drops off, in case it was unplugged or covered.
- **When nobody's home.** In Away mode, someone hanging around gets an urgent alert with their picture. Optionally, Doorstep can switch on the camera's light or say a warning through it.
- **Post, bins and bank holidays.** Doorstep learns when the postie usually comes and tells you when they're late. Bin day reminders the evening before. UK bank holidays from GOV.UK.
- **Desktop widget.** Your cameras' latest pictures and a line about today, in three sizes.

## Good to know
- Other cameras show live and send person alerts. History, recording and zones are still Ring-only.
- Ring Alarm sensors and Ring Smart Lighting aren't supported yet.
