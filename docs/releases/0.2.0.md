# Doorstep 0.2.0

## Know who it was
- **Couriers by name.** Doorstep reads DPD, Royal Mail, Amazon, Evri, DHL and other names off vans, jackets and bags, so a moment says "DPD left a parcel" instead of "Motion".
- **It learns your drivers.** Tell it "Who was it?" on any clip and it recognises them next time.
- **Parcel left, parcel taken.** The doorbell notices a parcel appearing on the step, and warns you if one disappears.
- **Optional cloud check.** Add your own Anthropic key and Doorstep can ask Claude about clips it can't work out. Off unless you switch it on, with a daily limit.

## Parcels
- **Everything you're expecting, in one place.** Paste a tracking number or a delivery email, or drag emails in. History now has a Parcels list.
- **Deliveries matched to the clip.** When a parcel is delivered, Doorstep links it to the moment it arrived so you can watch it.
- **Read delivery emails automatically (optional).** Connect an Outlook.com or Microsoft 365 mailbox, read-only.

## Telling you
- **Notifications with a picture** that say who: "Royal Mail rang", "Parcel taken".
- **Daily brief.** One line on your day: deliveries, rings, visits, and whether the night was quiet. Optional evening notification.
- **Keep clips.** Copy people and doorbell clips into a folder of your choice so they outlive Ring's history.

## Updates
- **Doorstep now updates itself.** New versions are checked, verified against Doorstep's signature, and installed for you. Settings › General › Updates.
