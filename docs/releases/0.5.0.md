# Doorstep 0.5.0

## Instant
- **Rings pop up straight away.** Doorstep now receives Ring's push alerts the same way Ring's phone app does, instead of checking every few seconds. If the connection drops, it falls back to checking until it's back.

## Smarter alerts
- **Zones.** Draw the part of each camera that matters (your path, your drive). People on the pavement and passing cars stop setting things off. Settings › General › Cameras › Zone…
- **Loitering.** "Someone's been hanging around the side gate for 40 seconds."
- **On your phone.** Doorstep's alerts, with the picture, on your iPhone or Android through ntfy (no account), Pushover or Telegram.
- **What visitors said** (optional): speech in doorbell clips turned into text on your Mac, and searchable.
- **Local AI** (optional): if you run Ollama, a vision model on your own computer describes every visit. Nothing leaves your home.

## Your Ring kit
- **Battery saver** (on by default): battery cameras show their latest picture until you click them, so opening Doorstep doesn't drain them.
- **Camera health:** Wi-Fi signal and battery for every camera, and a nudge when a battery's low or a camera goes offline.
- **Chimes:** snooze for an hour, three hours or until tomorrow, or play a test ring.
- **Ring's own modes:** when Doorstep switches to Away, Ring and Ring Alarm can follow.

## Your smart home
- **Home Assistant and Homebridge.** Who came, parcels, snapshots, battery and the mode appear in Home Assistant by themselves over MQTT (your own broker). Home Assistant can switch Doorstep's mode too.
