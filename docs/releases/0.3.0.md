# Doorstep 0.3.0

## Record without a Ring subscription
- **Your Mac is the recorder.** Choose cameras in Settings › Recording and Doorstep keeps them live, spots people itself, and saves every visit with the few seconds before it. No Ring Protect needed.
- **Time-lapse for battery cameras.** A still every few minutes, turned into a video of the day.
- **History plays your recordings** when Ring has no clip.

## Command centre
- **Everything on one screen** (⌘K): every camera large, people boxed as they move.
- **Map:** your home from above with each camera where it really is, and trails when someone walks round the house.
- **Radar:** live blips for people the cameras can see right now.
- **Insights:** busiest hours, the postie's usual time, and anything out of the ordinary.
- **Float a camera** on top of your other windows.

## Smarter
- **Someone walking up to the door** opens the doorbell before they ring.
- **Knocking, dogs, breaking glass and alarms**, heard in your clips.
- **Number plates** (off by default): name your car and Doorstep tells you when it's home.
- **Search** History in plain English: "DPD last week", "knock", "person at the garage yesterday".
- **Your day in 60 seconds:** a short video of the day's visits.

## Parcels
- **"I'm expecting a parcel":** add a tracking number, get a reminder on the day, and the door shows itself when someone arrives.
- **Live tracking** with your own free 17TRACK key, a Track button for every carrier, and Add to Calendar.
- **Rain warning** if a parcel is left out in the rain.

## Works with your Mac
- **Automations:** run any Shortcut when the doorbell rings, someone arrives, or a parcel is left (porch lights, music, messages).
- **Siri and Shortcuts:** "Show my door in Doorstep", "What happened at home in Doorstep".
- **Spoken announcements** in a British voice (optional).

## Privacy
- **The microphone is only on while you hold Talk.** Watching and recording never use it.
