# Getting started

## What you need
- A Mac with **macOS 14 Sonoma** or later (Apple silicon or Intel).
- A **Ring account** with at least one doorbell or camera. A Ring subscription is optional.

## 1. Download
Go to the **[latest release](https://github.com/iinde/doorstep-releases/releases/latest)** and download the file ending in **.dmg** (for example `Doorstep-0.3.1.dmg`).

## 2. Install
Open the downloaded file. A window appears with Doorstep and your Applications folder. **Drag Doorstep onto Applications.** You can then eject the disk image (the eject button next to "Doorstep" in Finder's sidebar) and delete the .dmg.

## 3. Open it the first time
Open **Applications** and double-click **Doorstep**.

Because Doorstep isn't notarised by Apple (that needs a paid developer account), macOS shows a warning the first time:

1. Click **Done** (or **OK**) on the warning.
2. Open **System Settings** › **Privacy & Security**.
3. Scroll down to the **Security** section. You'll see *"Doorstep was blocked..."*. Click **Open Anyway**.
4. Confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID, then click **Open**.

You only do this once. [Why?](../../FAQ.md#why-does-macos-say-it-cant-check-doorstep)

## 4. Find it in your menu bar
Doorstep doesn't sit in the Dock. Look at the **top right of your screen**, near the clock, for a small doorway icon. Click it.

Can't see it? On MacBooks with a notch, the menu bar can run out of room and hide icons. Quit a menu bar app or two, or press **⌘`** (Command and the key above Tab) to open Doorstep from anywhere.

## 5. Sign in to Ring
Type the **email and password you use for Ring** and click **Continue**. Ring then sends you a **six-digit code** (by text, email or your authenticator app, depending on your Ring settings). Type it in and click **Verify**.

Your cameras appear within a couple of seconds.

## 6. Allow notifications (recommended)
macOS asks whether Doorstep can send notifications. Say **Allow** so you hear about the doorbell, visitors and parcels. You can fine-tune these later in **Settings**.

## What next?
- Click any camera to see it big, talk, or turn on its light. ([Watching your cameras](watching.md))
- Open **History** to see everything that happened today. ([Who was it?](who-was-it.md))
- Click the **box icon** to tell Doorstep about a parcel you're expecting. ([Parcels](parcels.md))
- No Ring subscription? Turn on **Settings › Recording**. ([Recording](recording.md))
- Press **⌘K** for the Doorstep window: live, history, map, radar and insights. ([The Doorstep window](command-centre.md))

## Updates
Doorstep checks for updates every few hours. When there's a new version you'll see what's new and an **Install Update** button. You can also check now from the **gear menu › Check for Updates…**, and change this in **Settings › General › Updates**.
