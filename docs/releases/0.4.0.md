# Doorstep 0.4.0

## Everything Ring Protect does, on your Mac
- **Choose what alerts you**: doorbell, people, parcels, vehicles, animals, familiar faces, unusual activity, or any movement. Settings › Alerts.
- **One alert per visit.** Later clips of the same visit update the alert instead of sending new ones.
- **Every alert says what happened**, in a plain sentence: "A DPD courier walked up to the front door, rang the doorbell and left a parcel."
- **Home, Away, Night and Quiet modes**: each decides which cameras alert you and which record. Switch from the Wall, on a schedule, or say "Set Doorstep to Away".
- **Familiar faces** (beta, off by default): name the people you know, and get "Mum is at the front door". Only faces you name are kept, and recognition happens on your Mac.
- **Unusual activity alerts**: someone at an hour that's rare for that camera.
- **24/7 recording** on wired cameras, in 10-minute files.
- **Keep recordings longer**: up to a year, or until you delete them.
- **Share** any clip from History.
