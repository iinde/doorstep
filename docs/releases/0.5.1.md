# Doorstep 0.5.1

## New
- **Search by what's in the picture.** Type "dog", "red van", "man in a hat" or "pushchair" in History and Doorstep finds visits that look like it, even though nothing was written down. It uses Apple's MobileCLIP on your Mac (switch it on in Settings › Intelligence; a one-off 108 MB download). Pictures never leave your Mac.
