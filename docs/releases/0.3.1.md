# Doorstep 0.3.1

## New
- **Try Doorstep without a Ring account.** Demo mode shows a made-up home with drawn cameras, two weeks of visits and parcels. Open Terminal and run: `open -a Doorstep --args -DoorstepDemo YES`. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
- **Quit, Settings and Check for Updates** are now in the gear menu on the Wall. ⌘Q quits.

## Better
- **Parcels list** is tidier: tracking numbers fit on one line and buttons no longer get cut off.
- **"I'm expecting a parcel"** button stays visible when the window isn't in front.
