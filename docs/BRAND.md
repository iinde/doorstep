# Doorstep: brand

## Idea
**The light left on.** Doorstep is the porch light you leave on for someone: warm, calm, there when you look. Not a security product that shouts. The brand is one image: a lit doorway at dusk.

## The mark
A rounded-top doorway, lit amber from inside, standing on a single step. Three shapes, nothing else.

- **Doorway**: an arch (rectangle with a semicircular top), proportion 1 : 1.45. Filled porchlight, warmer at the floor.
- **Step**: a bar wider than the door (1.5x), paper coloured, the same thickness as the gap between door and step.
- **Glow**: a soft amber halo behind the doorway. Only in full-colour uses.

It must read at 16 px: in the menu bar it is the doorway outline and the step, drawn as a template (macOS tints it). When someone needs a look (unseen visitor) a small dot sits top right, like every Mac menu bar badge.

## App icon
macOS squircle. Night sky gradient (slate at the top to night at the bottom), the glowing doorway centred slightly low, step beneath with a faint wedge of light spilling down from the door onto it. No text, no bell, no camera.

## Wordmark
"Doorstep" in SF Pro Display Semibold, tracking -1%, paper on night or night on paper. The mark sits left of the word at cap height x 1.3. Never in all caps.

## Colour (unchanged from DESIGN-BRIEF.md)
night #0E0F11 · slate #1A1C20 · paper #F2EFE9 · ash #8A8D93 · **porchlight #F5B544** (the brand colour) · ember #E5484D (errors only).

## Voice
Plain, short, British, calm. "DPD left a parcel", not "Package delivery event detected!". No exclamation marks except the one Sparkle adds.

## Where it shows
App icon, menu bar, sign-in screen, Settings > General header, installer window, update window (via app icon), notifications (app icon).
