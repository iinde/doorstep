# The command centre

Press **⌘K** on the Wall (or click the grid icon) for a full window you can make full screen. It has four views; switch with the buttons at the top or **⌘1** to **⌘4**.

## Live
Every camera, large. When Doorstep spots a person, they're boxed; if they're walking towards the camera the box turns amber and says **Approaching**. Right-click a camera to **float** it on top of other windows.

The top right shows how many cameras are live, how many are recording, anyone in view right now, and parcels due today.

## Map
A satellite view of your home, centred on the address Ring has for it. Each camera has a cone showing what it can see.

1. Click **Place** next to a camera, then click where it really is on the map.
2. Use the slider to point it the right way.

When someone is seen on one camera and then another within a few minutes (driveway, then front door, then garden), the map draws their **trail**.

## Radar
Your home in the middle, each camera covering a slice (from where you placed it on the Map). **People your cameras can see right now** show as amber blips: left or right from where they are in the picture, nearer the middle the closer they are to the camera. The last hour's activity shows as fading dots.

Live blips come from cameras that are recording (Settings › Recording) or open in the command centre.

## Insights
Worked out from the last two weeks:
- **When things happen**, by hour, split into doorbell, people and other movement.
- **Every day**, the same split.
- **Deliveries by time of day**, coloured by carrier.
- **Busiest cameras.**
- **The postie's usual window**, once Doorstep has seen Royal Mail a few times.
- **Out of the ordinary**: visits at an hour that's rare for that camera.

## Tips
- Put the command centre on a second screen, or send it to a TV with AirPlay screen mirroring, for a proper security-desk feel.
- It uses more of your Mac's power than the menu bar view. Close it when you don't need it.
